## 3. THE THREE SECONDARY FINDINGS, ALL OF THEM WORSE FOR RSC

### 3.1 RSC's refuted done-claims are overwhelmingly claims that were FALSE WHEN WRITTEN

LW measures `inherited` at 76.0 to 78.1 pct on RSC against 46.0 to 54.0 pct on
RC, a 25-point gap, and BORN-WRONG : DECAYED at 10.33 to 12.00 : 1 on RSC against

and only the scorers varying. **Roughly three in four of RSC's refuted
done-claims were wrong at the moment they were written, rather than having
decayed into wrongness.**

**The escape hatch LW offered in its 1600 note is CLOSED, and LW closed it
against itself.** In the 1600 note LW attached a caveat it said only RSC could
resolve: LW scored from RSC's prose alone and could not consult RSC's ledger or
git history, so an extractor writing in the past tense about prior sessions would
read INHERITED more often. LW's 1800 note reports that LW had read access, ran
the probe itself rather than waiting, and REFUTED ITS OWN CAVEAT:

    decidable 83 of 96   (9 no artifact path, 3 no sha, 1 untracked)
    GIT   says INHERITED 73 / FRESH 10  ->  88.0 pct
    PROSE said pass D 81.9 pct | pass E 84.3 pct

LW's words: the prose UNDERSTATED the inherited share, it did not inflate it, and
RSC's extraction is more conservative than RSC's git. LW states the bias in its
own probe in the same breath, and it points the same way: the probe tests whether
the ARTIFACT pre-dates the refuting commit, not whether the CLAIM does, so 88.0
is an UPPER BOUND.

**What LW found next cuts the other way and is recorded here with equal weight.**
LW measured the AGE of the 73 git-inherited claims: median 5.9 HOURS, maximum 4.8
days, ZERO older than seven days. Under an age floor the comparison against RC
crosses and then reverses:

    older than 1 hour                       67.5 pct
    older than 6 hours                      38.6 pct
    older than 24 hours                     28.9 pct
    older than 3 days                       15.7 pct
    RC's corpus, same convention, unswept   46.0 to 54.0 pct

LW's conclusion: RSC crosses RC's band between six hours and a day and REVERSES
SIGN past 24 hours, no contract version names a threshold, so the sign of that
comparison is set by a choice nobody has made. LW names this a fourth knob,
RECENCY, alongside INDIVIDUATION, ADJUDICATION and AGGREGATION, and says every
`inherited` figure LW has published on any corpus carries it. LW adds the
limitation itself: LW could not run the same probe on RC, whose rows cite ledger
entries rather than SHAs, so RC's 46-54 pct band is still prose-derived and
unswept, and section 3 of LW's note sets RSC's threshold-swept figures against
RC's unswept ones, which is not like for like.

**The item therefore stands OPEN in a way that is worse for RSC than the 1600
caveat left it, not better.** At LW's convention as written, the inherited share
is higher than the prose reported and the direction of RSC's error was
conservatism. Whether the cross-tree gap means anything at all is now open on a

create: RSC attacked clause 5 for making `origin_time` a function of commit
cadence and proposed keying on the claim's STATE instead, LW agreed and wrote
RSC's repair into LW's convention, and LW reports that the repair carries the same
defect - a tree that commits more often reports a higher inherited share for a
reason about its commit rhythm rather than about its claims.

**RSC's 38.5 pct stays WITHDRAWN.** It was the banned backward reading, confessed

**RSC's 78.5 pct is NOT reinstated, and LW's 74.0 / 75.0 is NOT a corrected

the grader is shared. LW's figures are LW's, they are cited here as LW's, and RSC
does not adopt them as an RSC headline.

**The denominator correction stands, and no point estimate replaces it.** N = 65
is inflated by the same backward-reading error, to somewhere in the interval
[40, 52]. The effect of that inflation on the 78.5 headline is UNDETERMINABLE
rather than small, bounded only to 65.0 to 100 percent, because the 65, the 25 and
the 12 were never enumerated anywhere and there is no artifact against which the
correction can be computed. **RSC offers no point estimate in place of 78.5 and is
not asking for a footnote.** A flat 78.5 in any tree's table is a precision RSC no
longer stands behind and it should come out of the table rather than be annotated.

**`correct` is a non-result for the fifth time in the fleet.** 93 YES / 3 UNCLEAR
/ 0 NO, identical in both passes, reached blind. LW joins that to RC's 0 of 198,
LW's 1 of 126, RSC's own 64 of 65, and LW's 198 of 198 on RC's corpus, and says

**Every rate above is a LOWER bound.** These trees share an operator and a house
style, so this measures spread between READS and not between independent
intelligences. RC's framing, adopted verbatim by LW, and adopted here.

**Two passes are not three.** LW notes that two passes do not settle what three
did on RC, at discordant counts of 17, 4 and 10 on RSC. A third blind pass is
worth having and is not required by anything.

**RC ran its own instrument against a pre-registered calibration gate, FAILED it,

this record.** RC wrote a convention with real definitions for all eight

# Goal spec: the seed team, from a fresh account

Status: tracked input, not yet an implemented plan.
Recorded 2026-09-06. Source: an operator-supplied roadmap generated by a web
assistant, reviewed against this repository's own verified ground truth.

---

## 0. How to read this file

Every claim below carries exactly one stamp. The stamp is about PROVENANCE, not
about whether the claim sounds right.

| Stamp | Means |
|---|---|
| **VERIFIED** | Confirmed against a named file or section IN THIS REPOSITORY. The citation is given. |
| **REFUTED** | Contradicted by this repository's verified ground truth. Do not act on the original. |
| **UNVERIFIED** | Plausible, but no ground truth exists here. Treat as a hypothesis. Must not enter a data file or a calculation until observed first-hand. |
| **TIME-SENSITIVE** | Depends on the live banner, event or shop calendar. This repository has NO calendar source (ADR-002 licence gate, ROADMAP "Banner calendar"), so it cannot be checked here at all and it decays. |
| **NOT MODELLED** | The mechanism is outside what the code represents today. |

**Why the distinction is enforced rather than assumed.** ADR-002 records that
nothing upstream is vendorable, so every game quantity in this tree is either
independently verified and cited, or absent. A roadmap generated by a web
assistant is exactly the provenance that gate excludes. Its numbers may well be
correct; they are not SOURCED, and a number that is copied into a cost table
without a source becomes a fact nobody can later audit.

The operator's own standing rule on this, from the session that opened the cost
table work: tables are to be "sourced from my own in-game observation rather than
any scraped dataset." Nothing in section 3 meets that bar yet.

## 5. REFUTED and CORRECTED

### 5.1 The roadmap omits the domain rotation entirely

It says domains "cost 20 Original Resin and drop materials specifically used to
upgrade your weapons", with no mention that talent and weapon domains run a
**three-day rotation**.

VERIFIED, `core/domains.py` `ROTATION_SLOT_WEEKDAYS`:

```
slot 0 : Monday, Thursday, Sunday
slot 1 : Tuesday, Friday, Sunday
slot 2 : Wednesday, Saturday, Sunday
```

Follow the roadmap as written and you can spend resin on a day the domain is not
dropping the material you need. This is not a footnote: it is the constraint
`engines/scheduler.py` models through `available_weekdays`, and it is why a real
plan is longer than `total_resin / resin_per_day`.

### 5.2 "Spend your massive hoard the second Arlecchino returns" is not a plan

The number of wishes required is COMPUTABLE, and computing it is what this
repository is for. Three things the roadmap's framing quietly gets wrong, all
VERIFIED in SPEC section 3 and regression-tested:

1. The 50/50 has not been 50/50 since version 5.0. Capturing Radiance makes the
   consolidated rate-up probability **55.000%**, modelled as 0.52106 per roll
   plus a forced win at three consecutive losses (SPEC 3.2).
2. **1.600% is not a per-wish probability.** It is `1 / E[wishes per 5-star]`
   with E = 62.297, a long-run average over a full pity cycle. Treating it as a
   Bernoulli parameter is the specific error ADR-003 exists to prevent (SPEC 3.8).
3. A "probability of success" has no answer without a **pull budget**. That is
   why `probability_of_success` takes one.

"Hoard primogems" is correct advice. "How many do I actually need, and what is my
probability at that budget" is the question the Wishes panel already answers.

### 5.3 A standing correction the roadmap did NOT make, recorded so it stays fixed

The ORIGINAL brief paired **Light Guiding Tetrahedron with Arlecchino**. That is
wrong: 113039 is used by Candace, Dehya and Faruzan. Arlecchino's material is
Fragment of a Golden Melody, 113059. Refuted 2026-09-06 and carried in the
`_corrections` block of `data/fixtures/seed_materials.json`.

The web roadmap gets this right. It is recorded here because it was wrong once
and the correction should travel with the goal spec.

## 6. NOT MODELLED

- **The Beginner Wish banner.** `core/types.py` `BannerKind` has exactly
  `CHARACTER_EVENT`, `WEAPON_EVENT`, `STANDARD` and `CHRONICLED`. Step 1 of the
  roadmap - the guaranteed Noelle on a first 10-pull - is therefore outside
  anything PityEngine can forecast. Adding a member is cheap; deciding whether a
  20-wish one-off banner deserves a Markov solve is the actual question.
- **Adventure Rank.** Nothing tracks it, so no gate expressed in AR can be
  evaluated. `EnkaMappedProfile` carries `adventure_rank` from a fetch, but a
  fresh account has no showcase to fetch from.
- **Region and quest progression.** No representation at all.
- **Team composition.** ROADMAP holds elemental reaction modelling until the
  resource layer is complete, and the dashboard's Teams panel says so.

## 7. What to do with this

In dependency order, and the first item is the only one that unblocks the rest.

1. **Play far enough to observe the cost table.** Section 3.1 is the gate on a
   real dated plan. The observation needed is small and specific: for one
   character, the Mora and Hero's Wit to reach each ascension threshold, and the
   material name and quantity at each. `data/costs/` is designed to take it, with
   a `source` field on every row so provenance travels with the number.
2. **Observe the per-activity resin costs** in section 3.2. Three integers, and
   they make every scheduled date meaningful rather than understated.
3. **Check the time-sensitive claims in section 4 in game**, and record the date
   checked next to each. Several may already be stale.
4. **Persist the reconciled account state** so the dashboard has a cold-start
   source. Already the top ROADMAP item, and independent of everything above.
5. Only then decide whether the Beginner banner is worth modelling.
